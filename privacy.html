Privacy policy

Where Can I Fly ("we", "the app") helps you find places to fly to by budget, travel style and passport. We collect as little as we can. This policy covers the app and this website.

What you can use without an account

You can explore destinations, prices, visa information and city guides without signing up. Your choices, such as departure city, passport, dates, budget and vibes, stay on your device.

If you create an account

You can sign in with Apple, Google or email to save the places you have been and your trip plans. We then store your email address or sign-in ID and the places and plans you save, so they are there on your other devices. We do not sell this information.

Travel partners and booking links

When you tap to check flights, hotels or tours, you leave the app or this website and go to a partner site (for example Aviasales, Trip.com, Agoda, Klook or GetYourGuide) through our partner network, Travelpayouts. That site's own privacy policy applies there. Some of these links are affiliate links: we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Partner sites may use cookies to know the booking came from us. We do not see your payment details.

Ads

The free version of the app may show ads from an ad network such as Google AdMob. The ad network may use a device advertising ID to show and measure ads. You can limit ad tracking in your phone's settings. Pro removes ads once that feature is available.

Purchases

Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment, and we only receive confirmation that you have Pro.

Crash and usage data

We may collect anonymous crash reports and basic usage numbers to fix bugs and improve the app. These do not identify you.

This website

This site does not set its own cookies or use tracking. Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Your choices

  • You can use the app without an account.
  • You can delete your account and saved data at any time in the app, or by contacting us.
  • You can ask us what data we hold about you.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us.
