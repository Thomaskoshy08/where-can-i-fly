Festivals and holidays

What's on in Valencia through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Mar15

    Las Fallas, 15 to 19 March

    Giant sculptures, fireworks and they burn them all on the last night. Very loud and busy.

  • Aug

    La Tomatina in Buñol (last Wednesday of August)

    The tomato fight, an hour from the city.

  • Aug

    August heat

    Hot and humid. Some local places close for holidays.
