Festivals and holidays

What's on in Dallas through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Jul4

    Fourth of July fireworks

    Fireworks across the city. Hot, often over 35°C.

  • Oct

    State Fair of Texas (late Sep to Oct)

    Huge fair at Fair Park with fried food, rides and Big Tex.

  • Nov

    Thanksgiving and Cowboys game (4th Thursday)

    Flights are full and pricey. Many shops close on the day.
