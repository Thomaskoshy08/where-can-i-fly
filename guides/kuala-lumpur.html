Festivals and holidays

What's on in Kuala Lumpur through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Jan

    Thaipusam at Batu Caves (date moves, Jan or Feb)

    Huge Hindu procession up to Batu Caves. Amazing to see, very crowded.

  • Mar

    Hari Raya Aidilfitri (date moves)

    Many shops and food stalls close for a few days and roads are busy.

    Plan around this

  • Aug31

    Merdeka Day, 31 August

    National Day parade and fireworks around Dataran Merdeka.

  • Dec

    Christmas and year-end sales

    Malls in Bukit Bintang light up with decorations and big year-end discounts.

  • Dec

    New Year's Eve countdown

    Fireworks and countdown parties at KLCC and Merdeka 118. Book rooms early.
