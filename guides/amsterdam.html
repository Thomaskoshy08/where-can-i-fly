Festivals and holidays

What's on in Amsterdam through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Apr

    Tulip season (late March to mid-May)

    Keukenhof gardens and the flower fields outside the city are in bloom.

  • Apr27

    King's Day, 27 April

    The whole city wears orange, with street markets and boat parties on the canals.

  • Dec

    Amsterdam Light Festival (Dec to Jan)

    Light art along the canals, best seen from a boat.
