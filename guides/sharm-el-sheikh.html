Festivals and holidays

What's on in Sharm el Sheikh through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Mar

    Ramadan (date moves, Feb or March)

    Resorts run as normal; some local cafés close in the day.

  • Jul

    Summer heat (June to August)

    Often over 40°C. Snorkel in the morning.

    Plan around this

  • Dec

    Winter sun season (Nov to March)

    Warm days and calm sea. The busiest time for Europeans.
