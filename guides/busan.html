Festivals and holidays

What's on in Busan through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Jul

    Beach season (Jul to Aug)

    Haeundae beach is packed and lively.

  • Sep

    Chuseok (date moves, Sep or Oct)

    Many shops close for 3 days and trains sell out.

    Plan around this

  • Oct

    Busan International Film Festival (Oct)

    Asia's biggest film festival in Haeundae.

  • Nov

    Busan Fireworks Festival (Nov)

    Huge fireworks over Gwangalli Bridge.
