Festivals and holidays

What's on in Cologne through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Feb

    Cologne Carnival (Feb or March)

    Huge street party from Weiberfastnacht to Rose Monday. Everyone dresses up.

  • Nov

    Christmas markets (late Nov to Dec)

    Seven markets, including one under the cathedral.

  • Nov11

    Carnival season opens, 11 November

    At 11:11 the old town fills with people in costume.

Good to know

Paying

Cash is still common in beer halls and small shops. Carry some euros.

Duty-free alcohol

From outside the EU: 1 litre of spirits, 4 litres of wine and 16 litres of beer
