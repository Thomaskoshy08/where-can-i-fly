Festivals and holidays

What's on in Las Palmas through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Feb

    Carnival (February or March)

    One of Spain's biggest carnivals, with drag queen gala.

  • May

    Pride Maspalomas (May)

    Big LGBTQ+ festival with parties.

  • Dec

    Winter sun high season

    Busy with Europeans escaping winter. Book early.
