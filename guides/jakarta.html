Festivals and holidays

What's on in Jakarta through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Jan

    Rainy season peak (Jan to Feb)

    Heavy rain can flood roads and slow traffic badly.

    Plan around this

  • Mar

    Lebaran, the end of Ramadan (date moves)

    Many businesses close for about a week and transport is packed.

    Plan around this

  • Jun

    Jakarta Fair (mid-Jun to mid-Jul)

    Huge month-long fair with concerts, food and shopping at Kemayoran.

  • Aug17

    Independence Day, 17 August

    Flags everywhere, street games and parades across Jakarta.

  • Dec

    Christmas and year-end sales

    Malls in Jakarta light up with decorations and big year-end discounts.
