Festivals and holidays

What's on in Guangzhou through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Feb

    Chinese New Year (date moves)

    Hundreds of millions travel. Trains and hotels sell out, many shops close.

    Plan around this

  • Feb

    Flower markets before Chinese New Year

    Night flower markets fill the streets before the new year.

  • Sep

    Mid-Autumn Festival (date moves, Sep or Oct)

    Lanterns along the Pearl River and mooncakes everywhere.

  • Oct

    Canton Fair (Apr and Oct)

    Huge trade fair. Hotel prices jump while it runs.

    Plan around this

  • Oct1

    Golden Week, 1 to 7 October

    Very crowded everywhere and prices jump.

    Plan around this
