Festivals and holidays

What's on in Xiamen through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Feb

    Chinese New Year (date moves)

    Hundreds of millions travel. Trains and hotels sell out, many shops close.

    Plan around this

  • Apr

    Qingming Festival, early April

    Short holiday. Trains and tourist spots get busy.

    Plan around this

  • Sep

    Mid-Autumn Festival (date moves, Sep or Oct)

    Locals play the mooncake dice game Bo Bing.

  • Oct1

    Golden Week, 1 to 7 October

    Very crowded everywhere and prices jump.

    Plan around this
