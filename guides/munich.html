Festivals and holidays

What's on in Munich through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Sep

    Oktoberfest (mid-September to early October)

    Millions visit for beer tents and rides. Hotels triple their prices, so book early.

  • Dec

    Christmas markets (late Nov to 24 Dec)

    Mulled wine and stalls at Marienplatz and across the city.

  • Dec25

    Christmas, 25 and 26 December

    Shops and most restaurants close.
