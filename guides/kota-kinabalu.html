Festivals and holidays

What's on in Kota Kinabalu through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Mar

    Hari Raya Aidilfitri (date moves)

    Many shops and food stalls close for a few days and roads are busy.

    Plan around this

  • Mar

    Mount Kinabalu climbing season (Mar to Sep)

    Drier months and the best chance of clear summit views.

  • May30

    Kaamatan harvest festival, 30 to 31 May

    Kadazandusun harvest festival with music, dancing and rice wine.

  • Nov

    Rainy season (Nov to Feb)

    Heavier rain. Mount Kinabalu climbs can be cloudy.

    Plan around this
