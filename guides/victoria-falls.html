Festivals and holidays

What's on in Victoria Falls through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Jan

    Rainy season (November to March)

    Afternoon storms and more mosquitoes. Take malaria advice.

    Plan around this

  • Apr

    Peak water (March to May)

    Huge spray. You may get soaked and see less.

  • Oct

    Low water (October and November)

    Parts of the falls run dry on the Zambian side. Best time for Devil's Pool.

Good to know

Paying

US dollars are used everywhere. Cards in hotels. Bring small notes.

Duty-free alcohol

5 litres of alcohol, including up to 2 litres of spirits
