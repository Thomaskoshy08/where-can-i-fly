Festivals and holidays

What's on in Manila through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Jan9

    Feast of the Black Nazarene, 9 January

    Millions join the procession in Quiapo. Avoid the area if you dislike crowds.

    Plan around this

  • Apr

    Holy Week (date moves, Mar or Apr)

    Many businesses close and people leave the cities.

    Plan around this

  • Aug

    Typhoon season (Jul to Oct)

    Heavy rain and flooding can stop traffic.

    Plan around this

  • Dec

    Christmas season (Sep to Dec)

    The Philippines celebrates Christmas for months. Malls go all out.
