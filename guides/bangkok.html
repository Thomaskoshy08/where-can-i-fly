Festivals and holidays

What's on in Bangkok through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Apr13

    Songkran, 13 to 15 April

    Thai New Year water fights fill the streets. Expect to get soaked and pay more for hotels.

  • Sep

    Rainy season (Jun to Oct)

    Short heavy storms. Streets can flood for an hour or two.

    Plan around this

  • Nov

    Loy Krathong (date moves, Nov)

    Candle baskets on the Chao Phraya and lakes at full moon.

  • Dec

    Christmas and New Year at the malls

    Giant light shows at ICONSIAM and Central World.

  • Dec

    New Year's Eve countdown

    Fireworks and countdown parties along the Chao Phraya River. Book rooms early.
