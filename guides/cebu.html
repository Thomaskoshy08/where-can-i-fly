Festivals and holidays

What's on in Cebu through the year, and when to plan around closures.

    Sinulog Festival, third Sunday of January

    A huge street parade. Book hotels early.

  • Apr27

    Re-enactment of the Battle of Mactan on the beach.

    Typhoon season (Jul to Nov)

    Storms can cancel ferries and island trips.
