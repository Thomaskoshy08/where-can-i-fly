Festivals and holidays

What's on in Phuket through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Apr13

    Songkran, 13 to 15 April

    Thai New Year water fights fill the streets. Expect to get soaked and pay more for hotels.

  • Jun

    Monsoon season (May to Oct)

    Rougher seas on the west coast beaches and red-flag swimming days.

    Plan around this

  • Oct

    Phuket Vegetarian Festival (date moves, Sep or Oct)

    Nine days of street processions and vegetarian food. Some parts are intense.

  • Nov

    Loy Krathong (date moves, Nov)

    Floating candle baskets on the sea and lakes at full moon.

  • Dec

    New Year's Eve countdown

    Fireworks and countdown parties on Patong and Kata beaches. Book rooms early.
