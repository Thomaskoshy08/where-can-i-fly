Festivals and holidays

What's on in Luxembourg through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • May

    Octave pilgrimage (spring)

    Two weeks of processions to the cathedral and a small fair.

  • Jun23

    National Day, 23 June

    Fireworks over the Pétrusse valley the night before and parties in the old town.

  • Nov

    Winterlights Christmas markets (late Nov to Dec)

    Markets on Place d'Armes and Place de la Constitution with a big wheel.
