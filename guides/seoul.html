Festivals and holidays

What's on in Seoul through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Feb

    Seollal, Lunar New Year (date moves)

    Many shops close for 3 days while families travel.

    Plan around this

  • Apr

    Cherry blossoms (early Apr)

    Yeouido and Seokchon Lake turn pink.

  • Sep

    Chuseok (date moves, Sep or Oct)

    Many shops close for 3 days and trains sell out.

    Plan around this

  • Nov

    Autumn leaves (late Oct to mid-Nov)

    Gyeongbokgung and Namsan in full colour.

  • Dec

    Christmas markets and Myeongdong lights

    Seoul goes all out on lights and year-end shopping.
