Festivals and holidays

What's on in Maldives through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Jan

    Best weather (Dec to Apr)

    Sunny, calm seas and the best visibility for snorkelling.

  • Mar

    Ramadan (date moves)

    Cafes on local islands close in the day. Resorts carry on as usual.

    Plan around this

  • Jun

    Southwest monsoon (May to Oct)

    More rain and wind, but also lower resort prices.

    Plan around this
