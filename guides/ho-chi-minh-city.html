Festivals and holidays

What's on in Ho Chi Minh City through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Feb

    Tet, Lunar New Year (date moves)

    Many shops and restaurants close for up to a week.

    Plan around this

  • Apr30

    Reunification Day, 30 April

    Long weekend. Domestic travel and hotels get busy.

    Plan around this

  • Jun

    Rainy season (May to Nov)

    Short heavy downpours most afternoons.

    Plan around this

  • Sep

    Mid-Autumn Festival (date moves, Sep or Oct)

    Lanterns and mooncakes, especially in Chinatown (Cho Lon).

  • Dec

    Christmas Eve on Nguyen Hue

    Huge crowds and lights in District 1.
