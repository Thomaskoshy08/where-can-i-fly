Festivals and holidays

What's on in Delhi through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Jan26

    Republic Day parade, 26 January

    Huge parade on Kartavya Path. Central roads close.

  • Mar

    Holi (date moves, Mar)

    Festival of colours. Expect to get covered in powder.

  • Oct

    Diwali (Oct or Nov)

    Festival of lights. Fireworks, busy markets, some offices close.

  • Nov

    Smog season (Oct to Jan)

    Air quality can be very poor. Bring a mask.
