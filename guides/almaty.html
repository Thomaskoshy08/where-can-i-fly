Festivals and holidays

What's on in Almaty through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Mar21

    Nauryz, 21 to 23 March

    Spring new year with yurts, food stalls and concerts across the city.

  • Jul

    Day of the Capital and summer hiking

    Trails to Kok-Zhailau and Big Almaty Lake are at their best.

  • Dec

    Ski season (December to March)

    Shymbulak is 30 minutes from the city and busy at weekends.
