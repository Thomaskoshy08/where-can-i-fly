Festivals and holidays

What's on in Siem Reap through the year, and when to plan around closures.

  • Apr

    Khmer New Year, mid-April

    Many shops and restaurants close for about 3 days.

    Plan around this

  • Apr

    Angkor Sankranta (Khmer New Year)

    Games, music and dancing around the Angkor temples.

  • Nov

    Water Festival (date moves, Nov)

    Boat races and celebrations on the river.

  • Dec

    Angkor Wat sunrise season (Nov to Feb)

    Coolest, driest months. Best time for temples, but busiest.
