About Where Can I Fly

Where Can I Fly is a travel app made in Singapore for travellers across Asia. It answers the question every long weekend starts with: where can I actually go, with my passport, on my budget?

Why we built it

Planning a short trip usually means a dozen tabs: flight prices, visa rules, what the weather is like, whether a festival will close everything. Where Can I Fly puts that in one place. Pick a budget and a vibe, and you see only the places you can enter and afford, with a simple guide to each one.

What we offer

  • Free travel guides to 361 places, with festivals and holidays by month.
  • A visa filter for all 199 passports.
  • Round-trip price estimates and booking through trusted partners such as Aviasales, Trip.com, Agoda, Klook and GetYourGuide.
  • Weekend and long-weekend shortcuts, and day-by-day trip plans.

How we make money

Where Can I Fly is free. We earn a small commission when you book through our partner links, at no extra cost to you, and offer an optional Pro upgrade. We never sell your data. See our privacy policy.

Our information

Guides are written by our team with AI help and checked against public sources. Visa rules and prices change, so we always point you to official sources before you book. Spotted a mistake? Tell us.
