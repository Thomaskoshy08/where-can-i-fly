What it does

Decide where to go in a minute, not an evening.

Budget slider

Set what you want to spend on a return flight, from S$100 to S$2,000+. The cheapest places show first.

Pick your vibes

Beach, food, nightlife, shopping, nature, spa and more. Choose a few and only the places that fit stay on the map.

Weekend trips

One tap for this weekend, next weekend or in two weeks. Friday to Sunday, dates already filled in.

Long-weekend finder

Public holidays turned into trips, with how many days of leave you need, if any.

Plan my trip

A day-by-day plan built from your vibes, with morning, afternoon and evening ideas. Shuffle it until it feels right.

Visa filter, 199 passports

See only the places you can enter visa-free, or add visa on arrival and e-visa. Clear colours, always with an icon.
